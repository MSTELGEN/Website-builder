# ManyRituals — Content Machine

A repeatable system for turning the *7-Night Sleep Reset* into short-form content
that stops the scroll, gets saved, and sends people to `manyrituals.vercel.app`.

> **The one job of every post:** make someone *save* it or *click the bio link* to
> get the free tracker. Saves + bio clicks → email subscribers → the product funnel.

---

## 0. The Strategy in One Page

**Product:** Free 7-Night Sleep Reset (web guide + interactive tracker). Email unlocks
the printable PDF, the printable tracker, and bonus Night 8.

**Why people subscribe:** they want the *tracker and their own data* — the gem. Content
exists to make them feel "I need to track this."

**The funnel:**
```
Hook (stop scroll) → Value (a sleep ritual / schedule) → Proof (research) →
CTA (save this + link in bio for the free tracker) → Site → Email → Product
```

**Three content jobs (rotate them):**
1. **SHAREABLE** — schedules, checklists, "save this" graphics. Goal: saves & shares.
2. **HOOK** — text-on-screen pattern-interrupts. Goal: watch time & stops.
3. **AUTHORITY** — research-backed myth-busting. Goal: trust → bio clicks.

**Golden ratio per week:** 3 shareable · 2 hook · 1 authority · 1 behind-the-scenes/story.

## 1. Hook Bank (text-on-screen, stop-the-scroll)

Use these as the first frame (0.0s). Big text, high contrast, centered. Hold 1.5s
before anything else moves.

### Curiosity / pattern-interrupt
- "Your bedroom is 3°C too warm. Here's the number."
- "The 90-minute rule that fixed my 3am wake-ups."
- "Stop counting sheep. Count this instead."
- "I tracked my sleep for 7 nights. Night 4 changed everything."

- "You're not bad at sleeping. Your room is."
- "This is why you wake up at 3am (it's not random)."
- "Melatonin gummies are the wrong fix. This is the right one."

### Loss / fear (use sparingly)
- "One drink before bed costs you 24% of your deep sleep." *(label as VERIFY — see §6)*
- "Your phone in the room is stealing your mornings."
- "Scrolling in bed isn't relaxing. It's the opposite."

### Promise / outcome
- "7 nights. 7 tiny changes. No supplements."
- "Fall asleep faster tonight — change one thing."
- "The free tracker that fixed my sleep in a week."
- "Save this sleep schedule. Thank me Friday."

### Relatable / callout
- "POV: it's 2am and your brain won't shut up."
- "If you 'sleep' but wake up tired, watch this."
- "Tired of being tired? Start here tonight."

### B) The 7-Night Plan (one ritual per slide / per day)
| Night | Ritual | One-line why |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Cool the room ≤19°C | Core temp must drop to fall asleep |
| 2 | Phone out of the bedroom | Removes the 2am scroll trap |
| 3 | Dinner 3h before bed | Digestion competes with deep sleep |
| 4 | Last drink 3h before bed | Alcohol fragments the second half |
| 5 | Warm shower 90 min before | The post-shower cool-down cues sleep |
| 6 | Breathe 4-in / 6-out | Longer exhale calms the nervous system |
| 7 | Sleep on your side | Helps some people breathe easier |

**Editing rules that win:**
- First frame readable with sound off. 80% watch muted.
- One idea per video. Don't teach all 7 nights in one Reel.
- Captions always on. Big, centered, high contrast.
- End on the CTA frame held 2s so they have time to tap.
- 9:16, safe margins (keep text out of the bottom 15% — UI covers it).

## 6. Evidence Discipline (don't break trust)

The whole brand is "no pseudoscience." Match the site's citation rules:
- **VERIFIED** claims (safe to state plainly): cool room helps sleep onset; alcohol
  fragments second-half sleep; longer exhale calms the nervous system; light/phones
  disrupt wind-down.
- **VERIFY** claims (hedge with "may/for some people," never cite a fake stat):
  exact percentages (e.g. "24% less deep sleep"), side-sleeping benefits, specific
  temperatures as universal rules.
- **Never** invent a study, a number, or a doctor quote. If you want a stat on screen,
  pull it from the `/references` page on the site, which is already vetted.
- When in doubt, say "research suggests…" not "studies prove…".
