```
Seven research-backed rituals for deeper sleep, calmer mornings, and a body that actually recovers — one per night, starting tonight. Built from published sleep science, not wellness trends.
```

**What you'll get:**
- A 10-page structured guide covering the 7 highest-leverage sleep variables, in the correct order to stack them

- The mechanism behind each ritual explained in plain language, with real research citations (Walker, Ebrahim, Spiegel, Haghayegh, and others)

- Adults who feel they're sleeping enough hours but still waking unrefreshed

- Anyone looking for a quick supplement recommendation — this guide does not promote supplements
- People with suspected sleep apnea, clinical insomnia disorder, or perimenopause-related sleep disruption — this guide addresses lifestyle and environment, not clinical conditions
- Anyone wanting a 30-day plan — this is a 7-night structured experiment, not a chronic condition treatment
- People who want personalised medical advice — we provide research synthesis, not diagnosis
