---

**How to fill in the variables:**

| Variable | What to put here |
|---|---|
| `[NAME]` | Commenter's first name, or omit if unknown |
| `[LANDING_PAGE_URL]` | Your live landing page URL |
| `[POST_TOPIC]` | The topic of the post they commented on |
| `[RELEVANT_TIP_FROM_POST_TOPIC]` | One specific tip from the post — see examples below |
| `Night [X]` | The night number that covers the post topic |

## Notes on Tone

- Don't open with "Hey girl" or "OMG thank you for commenting" — the brand voice is warm but not performative
- Don't make promises ("this will change your sleep forever") — the brand is honest about what the guide does and doesn't do
- Don't add hashtags to DMs
- The 🌱 sign-off is the brand signature — keep it in all versions
- If the commenter replies asking a question: answer it factually and briefly. If it's a clinical question (apnea, medication interactions, insomnia diagnosis), reply: "That's worth talking to a GP about — this guide covers lifestyle variables, not clinical conditions."
