**One thing the guide doesn't mention:**

The sock detail is one of those counterintuitive facts that tends to confuse people, so here's the full mechanism:

Your feet have a high density of arteriovenous anastomoses — specialised blood vessels that act as heat regulators. When your feet are warm, these vessels dilate and release heat outward. That heat redistribution is what drops your core temperature.

So wearing socks in a cool room doesn't make you *warmer* in a way that harms sleep. It makes your feet better at doing their job, which is to release heat on your behalf.

If your feet are always cold at bedtime, socks are not optional — they're functional.
